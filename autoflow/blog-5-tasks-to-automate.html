5 Tasks Small Businesses Can Automate

September 24, 20262 min readGuides

You don't need a complex system to benefit from automation. The best place to start is with the small, predictable tasks you already do every week. Here are five that most small businesses can automate.

1. Order and booking confirmations

Every customer expects a confirmation after they buy or book. Writing these by hand is slow and easy to forget on a busy day. An automated email can go out the moment an order or booking comes in, with the right details filled in.

2. Copying form responses into a spreadsheet

If you collect information through an online form and then retype it into a spreadsheet, that step can disappear entirely. New responses can be added to the right sheet, in the right columns, as soon as they arrive.

3. Creating and sending invoices

Invoices follow the same pattern every time: customer details, items, totals, and a due date. That makes them ideal for automation. When a sale or job is completed, the invoice can be generated and emailed without anyone opening a template.

4. Following up after a sale or appointment

A thank-you message and a review request can make a real difference, but they're often the first things skipped. A follow-up sequence sends each message on schedule, so every customer hears from you.

5. Weekly or monthly reports

If you build the same summary at the end of every week, the calculations can be automated. Totals update as new information arrives, so the report is ready when you need it.

Where to begin

Pick the task you repeat most often and start there. One well-built workflow is more useful than five half-finished ones. If you're not sure which to choose, the Automation Builder can help you map it out.
