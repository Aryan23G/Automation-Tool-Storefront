About Me

I am a Computer Science student interested in software development, web technologies, automation, and digital business.

AutoFlow was created as an e-commerce project exploring how software-based services can be offered and presented through an online business. Instead of selling physical products, AutoFlow sells automation setups — packaged services with clear descriptions, upfront prices, and a straightforward way to buy.

Why AutoFlow?

The idea behind AutoFlow came from the large number of repetitive digital tasks businesses perform every day — copying customer details between tools, building the same invoices, and sending the same follow-up emails.

By combining software development with automation, routine processes can be made faster, more consistent, and easier to manage. AutoFlow presents that idea as a set of services that non-technical business owners can understand and purchase with confidence.

About this project

This website was built with HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript as an academic e-commerce project. It demonstrates a product catalogue, product detail pages, a working shopping cart that saves to your browser, a demo checkout, a blog, and a privacy policy.

Academic demonstration. AutoFlow is a fictional company. No real services are sold and no real payments are processed.
