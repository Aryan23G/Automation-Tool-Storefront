Who we are

We're a group of Computer Science students who share an interest in software development, web technologies, automation, and the business side of tech.

We kept noticing the same pattern: people at companies spending hours on redundant tasks like retyping data from one tool into another, rebuilding the same invoice, or sending the same follow-up email by hand. A well-built workflow can handle that work on its own, and helping businesses get there is what AutoFlow is about.

What we're building

AutoFlow is the storefront for our automation services. Instead of selling physical products, we package automation setups as services, each with a clear description, an upfront price, and a straightforward way to buy.

Choose a package from our services or sketch your own workflow in the Automation Builder, add it to your cart, and tell us how the task is done today. We handle the technical side: building the workflow, connecting your tools, and testing everything before it goes live.

Why redundant tasks?

Small, repeated tasks rarely look like a problem on their own. Added up over a week, they cost hours, and every manual step is another chance for a typo, a delay, or a forgotten email.

Larger companies often have technical teams to connect their systems. Smaller businesses usually don't. We want to make automation something any business owner can understand and buy with confidence, without writing a line of code.

Where the store is today

We built AutoFlow with HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript as part of our e-commerce course. It includes a service catalogue, product detail pages, the Automation Builder, a shopping cart that saves to your browser, a checkout, a blog, and a privacy policy.

Checkout is in demo mode. The storefront is still being built, so no real payments are processed and nothing you enter at checkout is sent or stored.
