Privacy Policy

01About this website

AutoFlow is a fictional company. This website was created as an academic demonstration for a university e-commerce course. It is designed to show how an online service business could present and sell its products.

This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to information on this site. Because it is a demonstration, the site is intentionally built to collect as little as possible.

02Information collected

This website does not have a server-side database or user accounts. It does not intentionally collect, store, or transmit personal information to the site owner.

When you use the site, your web browser and the hosting provider may automatically handle standard technical information (such as your IP address, browser type, and pages requested) in order to deliver the pages. That handling is controlled by the hosting provider, not by this site's code.

03Contact forms

The contact form and checkout form are demonstrations. When you submit them, the information is checked in your browser for completeness and a confirmation message is shown. The information is not sent by email, not saved to a database, and not shared with anyone.

Please avoid entering sensitive personal information into any form on this site.

04Cookies

This site's own code does not set cookies, and it does not use advertising or analytics tracking.

The site loads the Inter typeface from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests the font files from Google's servers, which may receive technical information such as your IP address. If the site is hosted on a third-party platform, that platform may also use its own cookies; please refer to the platform's own privacy policy.

05Shopping cart data

The shopping cart uses your browser's localStorage to remember which services you have added, so your cart stays filled as you move between pages. Only the service identifiers and quantities are saved — no names, emails, or payment details.

This data stays on your device and is never sent to a server. You can remove it at any time by clearing your cart, completing a demo order, or clearing your browser's site data.

06Payment information

This website is an academic demonstration and does not process real payments.

The card number, expiry, and CVV fields on the checkout page are for demonstration only. They are checked for format in your browser, are cleared as soon as the demo order is placed, and are never stored or transmitted. No payment provider is connected to this site. Please do not enter real card details.

07Data usage

Because no personal information is collected by this site's code, it is not used for marketing, sold, or shared with third parties. Cart data saved in your browser is used only to display your cart and order summary.

08Data security

The best protection is not collecting data in the first place, and this site is built that way: forms are processed only in your browser and nothing is stored on a server.

This website does not claim any security certification or regulatory compliance. If the site is served over HTTPS, the connection between your browser and the host is encrypted by the hosting provider.

09Contact information

Questions about this privacy policy or this academic project can be sent through the Contact page or by email to [your.email@example.com].
